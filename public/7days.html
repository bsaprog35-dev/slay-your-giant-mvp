01 // THE REAL OBSTACLE

Why Good Intentions Still End in Comfort Drifting

DIAGNOSIS // SEC_A

Most people don't fail because they lack talent, faith, or desire. They stall because of comfort drifting—staying busy with low-risk routines while the single assignment that matters most gets pushed to "next week."

You don't need another three-hour seminar or a surge of hype that fades by Tuesday morning. Under Dr. Ben's AIM (Active Intentional Movement) principle, clarity follows motion—God does not align steps that aren't moving.

This 7-day plan strips away the noise. Each day gives you one direct prompt you can finish in ten minutes so you stop rehearsing excuses and start stacking documented wins.

"The greatest potential is at the graveyard—not because people lacked a calling, but because they let unnamed giants keep them standing still."

Confidence isn't the prerequisite for taking action—it's the result of taking a ten-minute step, adjusting one condition, and proving to yourself you follow through.
