01 // THE REAL OBSTACLE

Why Good Intentions Still End in Comfort Drifting

DIAGNOSIS // SEC_A

Most people don't stall because they lack talent, faith, or desire. They stall because of comfort drifting—staying busy with safe routines while the one assignment that matters most keeps getting pushed to "next week."

You don't need another burst of hype that fades by Tuesday morning. Under Dr. Ben's AIM (Active Intentional Movement) principle, clarity follows motion—God does not align steps that aren't moving.

This 7-day protocol strips away the noise. Each day gives you one direct prompt you can complete in ten focused minutes so you stop rehearsing excuses and start stacking documented wins.

"The greatest potential is at the graveyard—not because people lacked a calling, but because they let unnamed giants keep them standing still."

Principle // Active Intentional Movement
PATTERN 01 // THE RESTART LOOP

You keep restarting the same promise

You know what needs to change—a difficult decision, a personal discipline, a project, or a leadership step—yet every week ends with the exact same unfinished aim.

PATTERN 02 // THE UNNAMED BARRIER

When procrastination, fear, or perfectionism stays vague, it runs the room. Naming the exact moment and condition that trips you up shrinks it to something you can confront.

PATTERN 03 // WAITING TO FEEL READY

You've been waiting for perfect timing

Confidence isn't the prerequisite for taking action—it's the result of taking a ten-minute step, changing one condition, and proving to yourself that you follow through.
